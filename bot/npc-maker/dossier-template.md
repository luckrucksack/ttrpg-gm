# TTRPG NPC Character Sheet: Literary Build

*The standard dossier for every NPC Bot. One per NPC, answered in full, saved at
`campaigns/<campaign_id>/npcs/<npc-slug>/dossier.md`. The filled sheet ships into
the NPC's bot profile (`dossier.md` beside `SOUL.md`) and is the GM's deep
reference. The bot's `SOUL.md` is distilled from it.*

**Grounding rules**
- Every answer must be consistent with the published adventure material and with
  recorded play. Play canon is binding — never contradict what has been
  established at the table.
- Where the published material is silent, extend the character freely — that is
  the job of this sheet — but never contradict it and never invent new plot.
- Keep the character's knowledge as a character's knowledge: things the NPC
  does not know are marked, and they stay unknown to the NPC.
- Write for an actor, not for a wiki. Brief, concrete, human.
