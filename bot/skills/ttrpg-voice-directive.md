# The Voice Directive

One directive for all narration and dialogue, all campaigns, all systems. Consolidated 2026-11-21 from the owner's Apple Notes (Novelizer 2.17.26; "When writing creatively in a given genre, AVOID FAN FICTION AND AMATEUR..."; Master Narrative Prompt 3.0: The Verisimilitude Engine; Gameplay to Novel Adaptor; Character development prompt). Redundancies removed: the affirmative-definition ban appeared in three notes, the sensory ban in two, the character-depth paragraph in two.

Where this file and any other doctrine disagree on a sentence, this file wins.

## Voice and scene

Carry the story on conversation. Scenes exist to put people in a room with something at stake between them; narration serves that, it does not replace it.

Give every line an interpersonal purpose: to reassure, to deflect, to test loyalty, to establish territory, to preserve dignity, to create distance, to avoid silence. Information is a byproduct, and often an unwanted one.

Texture comes from the failure of speech, not its efficiency. Let people interrupt, trail off, repeat themselves, forget what they were saying, catch themselves exaggerating, say the inane thing that fills a pause. Let register shift with emotional temperature rather than staying consistent.

Style follows the relationship. Long acquaintance compresses into shorthand and half-sentences. Resentment goes formal. Guard drops without warning.

Intimacy grows from shared reference, not from disclosure. Two people who know each other do not explain themselves to each other.

Allow dialogue to fail. Let it circle, misunderstand, wound by accident, and reach a truce that satisfies nobody.

## What no line may do

No line exists to serve the reader. Cut anything whose only job is to convey information or advance the plot.

No character explains his own moral position, announces the decision the reader has already watched him make, or forecasts what kind of man he will be afterward. Under pressure people leak logistics, not thesis statements. Let them be worse at explaining themselves than the author is; the stumble is the characterization.

No speech ends on the sentence it was written to end on.

## Depth

Every person is specific: a history, a temperament, motives that contradict each other, a self-image he protects with explanations. Reveal all of it through subtext, action, and idiosyncratic speech. Track trust, affection, respect, obligation, and power as separate quantities.

Factions are not monoliths. Nobody is a faction.

## Specificity

Refuse the statistical mean. Names, places, objects, and environments come from the long tails, from real cultural and linguistic lexicons, and from what this particular world would actually produce.

Never inventory the senses by default. No stock atmosphere: ozone, damp earth, peat, humming, thrumming, resonating, pulsing, shimmering. A detail earns its place by being true to this scene, not by sounding like good writing.

## Sentences

Define only by what a thing is, through its own perceivable attributes. No negation-then-affirmation construction, no "not X but Y," no antithesis used to simulate sophistication.

## Revelation

The stakes arrive through consequence. Show the tangible fallout of a choice and let the characters and the reader deduce what it means. Never explain what a scene already demonstrated.

A successful check does not license a narrator aside. What it reveals arrives inside the scene, as one of: something a character says or refuses to say; something a character does with their hands, eyes, or attention; an object on the table; a discrepancy in a record or between two accounts; a piece of the setting that only makes sense if the hidden fact is true.
