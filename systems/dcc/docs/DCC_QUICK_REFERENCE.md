# DCC Quick Reference Guide
## Essential Mechanics for Play

### Spellburn
- Temporarily sacrifice STR, AGI, or STA for spell power
- Each point sacrificed = +1 to spell check
- Points return at rate of 1 per day of rest
- Cannot reduce ability below 3

### Corruption
- Occurs on spell failure
- Roll on Corruption Manifestations table
- Effects are usually permanent
- Minor: cosmetic changes, slight penalties
- Major: significant penalties, occasional benefits
- Extreme: debilitating, often character-altering

### Mercurial Magic
- Spellcasters roll dice chain when casting spells
- Dice chain: d3 → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 → d14 → d16 → d20 → d24 → d30
- Success moves up the chain, failure moves down
- Determines spell potency and special effects

### Luck System
- Luck is a resource that can be burned
- Burn Luck to add to any roll (after seeing result)
- Each point burned reduces Luck score permanently
- Luck score determines Luck modifier (like other abilities)
- Regain Luck through favorable outcomes and patron favor

### Dice Chain
- Used for Mercurial Magic and other random determinations
- Starts at d3, can go up to d30
- Up = better result, Down = worse result
- Cannot go below d3 or above d30

### Mighty Deeds (Warriors)
- Declare special attack effect before rolling
- If attack hits and deed die (based on level) shows 3+, effect occurs
- Deed die: d3 at levels 1-2, d4 at 3-4, etc.
- Effects: disarm, push back, knockdown, etc.

### Action Dice
- Based on class and level
- Determine number of actions per round
- Higher = more actions
- Used for attacks, spells, and other activities

### Critical Hits & Fumbles
- Natural 20 = roll on appropriate Crit table
- Natural 1 = roll on Fumble table
- Tables vary by weapon type and class

### Hit Points & Recovery
- HP = class die + Stamina modifier per level
- Recover HP through rest and healing
- First Aid: heal 1d3 HP once per day
- Healing magic: variable based on spell

### Saving Throws
- Roll d20 + relevant ability modifier
- Target number based on effect level
- Success avoids or reduces effect

### Ability Checks
- Roll d20 + ability modifier
- Compare to difficulty class (DC)
- Success if total meets or exceeds DC

### Combat Sequence
1. Initiative (Agility check)
2. Surprise round (if applicable)
3. Regular rounds:
   - Each character acts on their initiative
   - Move and action (or two actions)
   - End of round bookkeeping

### Healing & Recovery
- Natural healing: 1 HP per level per day of rest
- Healing magic: spell-dependent
- First Aid: once per day, 1d3 HP
- Significant injuries may require extended recovery

### Experience Awards
- Defeating enemies: based on enemy XP value
- Treasure: 1 XP per 1 gp value
- Story awards: DM discretion for roleplaying, goals achieved
- Training time: 1 week per level (can be reduced)

### Common Modifiers
- Cover: -2 to -5 to hit
- Range: varies by weapon
- Condition: based on situation
- Magical aids: spell or item dependent
- Fatigue: cumulative penalties
