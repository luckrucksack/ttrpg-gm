Research brief: AI Dungeon Master notes in Apple Notes¶

Goal: Find every note about AI Dungeon Masters / AI GMs, then synthesize a deduplicated picture of the DM and GM master prompts — with the prohibitions preserved in full, specific detail.

Phase 1 — Inventory¶

  • List every matching note: title, folder, modified date, and its role (draft / iteration N / canonical / fragment / test run).
  • Separate master-prompt notes from supporting material (behavior notes, failure fixes, test logs).

Phase 2 — Extraction¶

  • Pull the full text of every distinct master prompt version, tagged with note title + date so the iteration lineage is traceable.
  • Preserve verbatim. Do NOT clean, shorten, or "improve" — the iterations are deliberate and the final versions work.

Phase 3 — Repetition & lineage¶

  • Map what changed between versions and what stayed constant. Identify the canonical/final versions (the ones that "ended up working pretty well").

Phase 4 — Prohibitions (the crown jewels)¶

  • Extract EVERY prohibition across all notes, verbatim where possible, with source note.
  • Group by theme but keep exact wording — these were added because of observed failures and must stay specific.
  • Flag anywhere a later iteration weakened or removed a prohibition.

Phase 5 — Deliverable¶

  • One research artifact in ~/workspace/: inventory, canonical DM prompt, canonical GM prompt, full prohibition catalog, iteration notes.
  • Chat summary: what was found, where the canonicals live, and the prohibitions at a high level.
